Paydayly — Support

Need help with Paydayly, a purchase or reminders? We are happy to help.

Contact

Email: darenight.app@outlook.com

Please describe the issue and include your device model and iOS or Android version. Never send passwords, bank details or payment information.

Common questions

Purchases. If Premium does not appear, open Settings → Premium → "Restore purchases". Payments are handled by Apple (iPhone) or Google Play (Android). For refunds, visit Apple's Report a Problem site or Google Play refunds.

Your data. Everything you enter is stored only on your phone. Use Settings → Backup regularly: deleting the app without a backup deletes your data.

Reminders. If reminders do not arrive, check that notifications for Paydayly are allowed in your phone's Settings app.

Paydayly — Suporte

Precisa de ajuda com o Paydayly, uma compra ou os lembretes? Escreva para darenight.app@outlook.com com o modelo do aparelho e a versão do iOS ou Android. Nunca envie senhas, dados bancários ou de pagamento.

Compras. Se o Premium não aparecer, abra Ajustes → Premium → "Restaurar compras". Reembolsos são feitos pela Apple ou pelo Google Play.

Seus dados. Tudo fica só no seu aparelho. Faça backups em Ajustes → Backup: apagar o app sem backup apaga seus dados.
